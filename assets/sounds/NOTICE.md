# Sound pack provenance

`smp_bank.bin` holds one sample per instrument for all 31 Super Mario Paint instruments, converted from `src/resources/soundfonts/soundset3.sf2` of [Super Mario Paint](https://github.com/DC37/Super-Mario-Paint), revision `969e4f55743b3383b450c2a4f9b3a3c4a5e25828`. Per that project's Version History, the soundset is Mochi115's ("close to original SF2 from MPC2.0 plus some extras"), with higher-quality samples by Cyan. The samples derive from Nintendo's Mario Paint.

These sounds are **not covered by MPFM1's MIT code license**. Super Mario Paint's MIT license covers its software, not the sound data, and gives no separate Nintendo license; they are kept on the same footing as the stamps in `../NOTICE.md`. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo or M-VAVE. They can be swapped at any time: remove or replace `smp_bank.bin` (same format, `tools/adpcm.py`), build with `STAMP_BANK=placeholder` (original generated tones, MIT), or use `STAMP_WAV_DIR` with your own WAVs.

Conversion (`tools/make_smp_bank.py`, MIT; deterministic): the zone covering key 60 of each instrument's preset (else the nearest), resampled to 22,050 Hz mono (windowed sinc), initialAttenuation applied and levels matched by RMS (never past a 30000 peak, at most x4), IMA ADPCM in 256-sample blocks. Root key, fine tuning and cents per key come from the SF2 zone. Preset names are matched by name to the SMP instrument order (the SF2 programs of Coin and Piranha Plant are swapped relative to it). The release build uses this committed file; the SF2 is not needed to build.
